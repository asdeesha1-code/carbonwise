# CarbonWise Source Notes

CarbonWise uses source-labeled prototype cards and links rather than presenting local demo values as live reporting.

## NASA Climate

NASA defines climate change as a long-term change in average weather patterns and explains that recent changes are driven primarily by human activities, especially fossil-fuel burning that increases heat-trapping greenhouse gases. Source: https://science.nasa.gov/climate-change/what-is-climate-change/

## UNEP Emissions Gap Report 2025

UNEP's 2025 report explains that current pledges remain insufficient to meet the Paris Agreement temperature goals and emphasizes faster, larger emissions reductions. Source: https://www.unep.org/resources/emissions-gap-report-2025

## India Meteorological Department

The India Meteorological Department provides current weather, city forecasts, warnings, and climate services for India. CarbonWise's current city cards remain local demo values; production weather should use an approved IMD or weather-data API integration. Source: https://mausam.imd.gov.in/

## Product boundary

Weather, news, fund contributions, payment history, home-screen widgets, and notification delivery are represented as prototype surfaces unless a production integration is explicitly connected. The app should not describe demo values as live measurements or claim that a contribution has been processed.
